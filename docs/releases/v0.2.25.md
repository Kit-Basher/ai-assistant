Superseded by v0.2.26. The installed-browser smoke found that a concrete
resource-status request beginning with conversational “can you do” wording
could be misread as a general capability-list question. No mutation or data
loss occurred. v0.2.26 makes concrete domain evidence take precedence.

WP6 pre-reinstall candidate superseding v0.2.24.

  the live `models.scout` capability and its bounded discovery view.

  well as the equivalent RAM/VRAM terms.
- Retains the WP6 layered gate, diagnostics export, normal-user onboarding
  copy, portable backup/isolated restore proof, maintenance audit, and Ubuntu
  recovery preflight.
- Physical Ubuntu 24.04 fresh-install recovery remains deliberately unproved;
  this release may only be reported as the WP6 pre-reinstall gate.
